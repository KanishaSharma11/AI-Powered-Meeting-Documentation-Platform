1 Background & Objective

The International Maritime Organization (IMO) is a specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for regulating international shipping, enhancing maritime safety, preventing marine pollution, and addressing emerging challenges including greenhouse gas emissions, digitalization, and autonomous vessels. As a founding Member State, India participates at the highest levels across all IMO bodies, with its engagement managed through the International Cooperation (IC) Branch of the Directorate General of Shipping (DGS).

To strengthen this engagement, India has established Shadow Committees that mirror IMO structures, supporting the official delegation through coordinated preparation, research, and stakeholder consultation. Existing coordination mechanisms rely heavily on manual processes and dispersed communication channels, leading to inefficiencies, delays, and lack of traceability.

Objective: Develop a unified digital engagement platform — the IMO Strategic Engagement Platform — to streamline committee management, agenda and document preparation, stakeholder collaboration, feedback consolidation, and reporting across India's IMO engagement lifecycle.

2 Solution Overview

The proposed solution combines modern software engineering practices with enterprise-grade architecture principles to deliver a secure, scalable, and future-ready platform. By leveraging a microservices-based architecture, open-source technologies, cloud-native deployment, configurable security controls, and open APIs, the platform ensures flexibility, interoperability, and ease of maintenance throughout its lifecycle.

5 Cross-Cutting Enterprise Capabilities

In addition to the functional modules, the platform provides enterprise-wide capabilities that operate across all modules to improve governance, compliance, and institutional memory.

Enterprise Search & Knowledge Discovery

Centralized search across committees, meetings, agenda items, documents, interventions, correspondence groups, national positions, reports, and archived records — supporting metadata filtering, keyword search, and OCR-based indexing.

Stakeholder Organization Registry

Centralized registry of participating Ministries, Departments, Classification Societies, Industry Associations, Research Institutions, and other approved stakeholders, including profiles and participation history.

Institutional Knowledge Repository

Preserves committee history, interventions, national positions, working papers, and decisions — enabling knowledge reuse and policy continuity for future international engagements.

6 User Roles & Access Management

The platform implements a Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) framework so that users are granted access only to the information and functionality required for their responsibilities. All role assignments remain fully auditable.

RoleKey ResponsibilitiesAccess Level
Super User DGS / DDG International Cooperation Overall governance, system configuration, monitoring of committees, users, workflows, and reports. Full access across all modules and system configuration
Administrator Day-to-day administration of committees, meetings, users, agendas, documents, and tasks. Full operational management with limited system configuration rights
Delegation Leader Reviews consolidated feedback, approves draft interventions and documents, monitors delegation readiness. Review, approval, monitoring, and delegation-level oversight
Coordinator Manages committees, working groups, and correspondence groups; coordinates agenda and task allocation. Manage assigned committees, agendas, tasks, submissions, and collaboration workspaces
Convener Functional lead for a committee / working group; validates technical positions and coordinates discussions. Lead discussions, review submissions, manage issue registers
Member Reviews assigned agenda items, prepares comments, participates in discussions, completes assigned tasks. Assigned access only; no approval or administrative authority
Consultant / Support Staff Provides research, documentation, and drafting support; uploads supporting materials. Access limited to assigned committees, documents, and tasks
Ministry Reviewer Reviews agenda papers, interventions, and policy documents for ministry-level examination. Review and comment access only; no administrative privileges

7 Security & Compliance Framework

The security framework ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of application data while providing a secure, resilient, and compliant operating environment. The application is hosted exclusively on a MeitY-empanelled Cloud Service Provider in accordance with the MeghRaj Cloud Initiative.

  • Four logically segregated environments — Development, UAT, Production, and Disaster Recovery — each with independent access controls.
  • Architecture aligned with prescribed Recovery Point Objective (RPO) and Recovery Time Objective (RTO), supported by encrypted backups and periodic DR drills.
  • Information classification and access governance — documents categorized as Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted, or Sensitive — with watermarking, download controls, and archival policies.
  • Compliance with ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27017, ISO/IEC 27018, CERT-In guidelines, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.
  • Compliance with GIGW 3.0 acceptance criteria and WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards.
  • Three mandatory security audits — Pre-Go-Live, Mid-Term, and Final — with all critical/high observations resolved prior to certification.

9 Deployment Model

The platform will be deployed on a MeitY-empanelled cloud with a Primary Data Center in Mumbai and a Secondary Disaster Recovery site in Hyderabad, connected via asynchronous replication to ensure business continuity. Four segregated environments — Development, UAT, Production, and DR — support a secure and controlled application lifecycle, with periodic DR drills conducted after Go-Live to validate recovery procedures.
