1 Purpose & Scope

This Low-Level Design (LLD) document translates the Functional Requirement Specifications (FRS) for the IMO Strategic Engagement Platform into module-wise requirement detail, workflow-level process design, non-functional requirement acceptance criteria, and role-wise access and permission matrices. It is intended to guide detailed system design, development, and testing for each of the platform's ten core functional modules.

The document is organized into four parts: Module-Wise Low-Level Design, covering every requirement ID, sub-module, business rule, and priority across the ten functional modules; Detailed Workflow Design, covering the objective, actors, process description, steps, business rules, and outputs of each of the platform's thirteen key workflows; Non-Functional Requirements, detailing security, usability, performance, auditability, availability, and interoperability acceptance criteria; and the Access Rights and Roles & Permission Matrices, defining module- and permission-level access for each of the eight user roles.

3 Detailed Workflow Design

The following thirteen workflows define the end-to-end process design underpinning the platform's modules, from user onboarding through committee constitution, meeting execution, and long-term archival.

8.1

User Registration Workflow

Defines the process for onboarding users onto the portal, ensuring only authorized personnel from DGS, MoPSW, MMDs, research organizations, industry associations, consultants, and other approved stakeholders are granted access.

Actors

Super User (DDG – International Cooperation)
Administrator
Delegation Leader
Registered User

Outputs

Active user account
Assigned role(s)
Committee membership
User dashboard
Audit trail entry
Stakeholder organization mapping
User lifecycle status
User activity analytics dashboard

Workflow Description

Registration begins with identification and nomination of users; the request may originate from the Administrator, Coordinator, or Delegation Leader. External-organization users are validated by the Delegation Leader before activation. The Administrator then creates the account, assigns role(s), committee memberships, and access permissions. The user receives login credentials, verifies the account, changes the temporary password, configures MFA, and accepts the terms of use before accessing the assigned dashboard. All activities are recorded in the audit log, and every user is mapped to the Stakeholder Organization Registry.

Workflow Steps

  1. Identification and nomination of users
  2. Submission of user details by the Administrator
  3. Validation of external members by the Delegation Leader
  4. Approval of registration request
  5. Creation of user accounts
  6. Assignment of user role(s)
  7. Mapping of committee membership
  8. Mapping of user to the Stakeholder Organization Registry
  9. Configuration of account validity period and lifecycle status
  10. Assignment of delegated authority, where applicable
  11. Generation of login credentials
  12. User activation and first-time login
  13. Password change and MFA configuration
  14. Access to role-based dashboard
  15. Periodic review of access permissions
  16. Annual membership validation and renewal

Business Rules

  • Every user shall possess a unique User ID
  • One user may belong to multiple committees; users may hold multiple roles subject to approval
  • External users require Delegation Leader approval before activation
  • All registration activities are recorded in the audit log
  • User accounts support lifecycle states: Active, Suspended, Expired, Deactivated, Reactivated
  • Delegated authority is time-bound and auto-revoked on expiry
  • Committee memberships and access rights are reviewed and validated annually
8.2

Annual Committee Constitution Workflow

Governs the establishment and periodic reconstitution of IMO Shadow Committees, ensuring composition, leadership, and stakeholder participation are updated annually.

Actors

Super User
Administrator

Outputs

Updated committee repository
Committee membership list

Committee Charter Repository
Committee History Repository

Workflow Description

At the start of each annual IMO cycle, the Administrator initiates the constitution process — reviewing and updating the Assembly, Council, Committees, Sub-Committees, Working Groups, and Correspondence Groups. Internal members are nominated by the Administration; external experts are proposed and validated by the Delegation Leader. The Administrator then assigns leadership roles (Delegation Leader, Convener, Coordinator, Members), configures committee-specific permissions, and notifies members. The Committee Charter Repository (ToR, objectives, governance structure) and historical compositions, outputs, and attendance are preserved, and KPIs on participation, agenda completion, and submission timeliness are generated.

Workflow Steps

  1. Initiation of annual committee constitution
  2. Review of existing committee structures
  3. Creation or modification of committees
  4. Nomination of internal members
  5. Nomination of external experts
  6. Validation of external members
  7. Assignment of committee leadership and member roles
  8. Configuration of committee permissions
  9. Notification to committee members
  10. Creation and approval of Committee Charter (ToR)
  11. Mapping members to stakeholder organizations
  12. Configuration of performance indicators and attendance tracking
  13. Creation of Committee Output Repository
  14. Archival of previous composition, leadership history, and outputs

Business Rules

  • Committee structures remain configurable; a member may participate in multiple committees
  • Committee leadership is assigned by the Administrator; external members require validation
  • Every committee maintains an approved Committee Charter (Terms of Reference)
  • Committee outputs are linked to the respective committee repository
  • Attendance and participation records are maintained for all activities
  • Committee memberships are reviewed and validated annually

Workflow Description

The Administrator or authorized Coordinator creates a meeting by entering metadata such as title, committee, session number, venue, and dates. Relevant IMO agenda documents are uploaded and linked. Committee members, Conveners, Coordinators, and Delegation Leaders are associated with the meeting based on committee membership. The system then automatically generates the meeting workspace, document repository, task lists, and calendar entries, and notifies all participants.

  4. Assignment of participants

  6. Generation of document repository
  7. Calendar update
  8. Dashboard update
  9. Notification to participants

Business Rules

  • Every meeting shall possess a unique Meeting ID
  • Meetings shall belong to only one committee
  • Participants shall be selected from committee membership
  • Meeting documents inherit committee access permissions
  • Calendar updates occur automatically
8.4

Agenda Assignment Workflow

Defines the process through which agenda items received from IMO are distributed among committee members for analysis, document preparation, feedback, and intervention development.

Outputs

Workflow Description

Agenda items are entered manually or via bulk upload following meeting creation, and categorized by committee, subject area, and priority, with supporting documents and prior interventions linked. The Delegation Leader or Convener assigns ownership to one or more responsible members; Working Groups, Sub-Groups, or Correspondence Groups are formed for complex items. Each assignment carries ownership, timelines, related documents, and expected deliverables, with automated notifications and dashboard tracking of status (Draft, Under Review, Consolidated, Approved, Completed).

Workflow Steps

  1. Upload of agenda items
  2. Agenda categorization
  3. Linking of supporting documents
  4. Assignment of agenda owner
  5. Creation of Working Group or Correspondence Group (if required)
  6. Assignment of responsible members
  7. Definition of timelines
  8. Notification to members
  9. Monitoring of agenda preparation
  10. Status updates until completion

Business Rules

  • Every agenda item shall possess a unique Agenda ID
  • Each agenda item is assigned to at least one responsible member with one designated owner
  • Multiple members may collaborate on a single agenda item
  • Agenda status updates automatically based on workflow progression
8.5

Task Assignment Workflow

Defines the process for assigning, monitoring, and tracking activities related to agenda preparation, document drafting, stakeholder consultation, and meeting readiness.

Actors

Delegation Leader
Convener
Coordinator
Committee Members
Consultant / Support Staff

Outputs

Task assignment records
Task progress dashboard
Reminder notifications
Escalation alerts
Task completion reports

Workflow Description

Following agenda assignment, Coordinators or Delegation Leaders create tasks linked to agenda items, documents, or committee activities, capturing title, description, responsible individual(s), priority, due date, and deliverables. The system notifies assignees and updates dashboards; members update status and upload supporting documents. Coordinators monitor pending, completed, and overdue tasks; automated reminders and configurable escalation apply to overdue tasks, with task completion linked to overall agenda and meeting readiness.

Workflow Steps

  1. Selection of agenda item or document
  2. Creation of task
  3. Assignment of responsible member(s)
  4. Definition of priority and due date
  5. Linking of supporting documents
  6. Automatic notification to assignees
  7. Progress updates by members
  8. Automated reminders before due dates
  9. Escalation of overdue tasks
  10. Task completion and closure

Business Rules

  • Every task shall have a unique Task ID and one primary owner
  • Each task is linked to an agenda item or document; multiple users may be assigned
  • Task priorities and reminder frequency are configurable
  • Escalation occurs automatically when predefined conditions are met
  • Completed tasks become read-only unless reopened by an authorized user
